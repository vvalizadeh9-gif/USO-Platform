What changed

One rail became two: a module switcher plus a nav panel, so Reports and Admin stop competing with the daily queues for the same list.

What stayed

The teal signal palette, amber-pending / green-approved / red-problematic, tabular figures, and Vazirmatn for every Persian name.

The trade

The rounded panel costs about 90px of horizontal room. Wide tables — Work Items, CPM validation — need the nav panel collapsible, not decorative.
