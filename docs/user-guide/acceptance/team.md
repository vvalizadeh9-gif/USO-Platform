# Acceptance Filing: team baskets

For NWG planning (NWG R&D), CPG PSO, CPG rollout PM (CPG PMO), CPG power
and Managed service (MS).

When ICT or CRA rejects a village for a reason your team owns, the
coordinator puts it in **your basket**. **Acceptance › Filing** lists those
villages, from every region, oldest first. Home shows **Your basket** with
how many are over the SLA (14 days) and how many have no comment in 7 days.

## What you can do

- **Add a comment** about progress at any time. A village with no comment
  for 7 days is flagged red for everyone.
- **Fixed · back to contractor** (press **W**) when the problem is solved.
  Say what was fixed; the comment is required. The village goes back to the
  site contractor, who asks the authority again, and leaves your basket.

You cannot record an approval or a rejection, and you cannot move a village
to another basket; ask the coordinator if it is in the wrong one.

## Reading a case

The history on the right shows the drive-test approval, the send to ICT HQ,
the rejection with the authority's comment and letter, and every comment
since. **Now · in {your team} basket** shows how many days it has waited.
