# Acceptance Filing: coordinators and the PM

Filing shows every open ICT and CRA case in your regions (the PM sees all).
**Insights › Acceptance baskets** shows who holds what, and for how long.

## Three states

- **Waiting**: always in the site contractor's basket.
- **Rejected**: in the basket the rejection category names. Parked goes to
  the PM; NWG R&D, CPG PSO, CPG PMO, CPG Power and MS go to their team;
  Contractor goes back to the site contractor.
- **Approved**: done, and gone from the inbox.

## Recording a letter

Your Approved and Rejected saves land directly; no one else confirms them.
Every save needs a comment. A rejection needs its category; a verdict needs
the letter number, Shamsi date and file. Tick several villages to record one
letter for all of them.

## Confirming contractors' filings

A contractor's Approved or Rejected waits in **ICT validation** or **CRA
validation**. When you confirm a rejection, check the category the contractor
picked and change it if it is wrong; the case goes to the basket you confirm.
Returning a filing keeps the case with the contractor, and your reason is
written into its history.

## Changing a basket

On a rejected case, **Change basket** moves it to another category's basket.
A reason is required, and the change is written into the history with your
name. Backfilled rejections from before UEP-22 show **Needs category**: set
one for each.

## Acceptance baskets

Rows are baskets, columns time in basket (under 3 months, 3–6, 6–12, over a
year), then **No comment** and **Total**. Click a cell to list its villages;
**Open in Filing** opens the inbox filtered to that basket and age.
