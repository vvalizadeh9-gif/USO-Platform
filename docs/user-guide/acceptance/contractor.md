# Acceptance Filing: contractors

You are responsible for getting ICT and CRA approval for your villages.
Filing (**Acceptance › Filing**) shows what is in your basket.

## What you see

- **ICT | CRA** at the top switches the authority. Each village has one ICT
  case and one CRA case.
- **Your inbox** lists villages *Waiting for ICT* (or CRA): these are yours
  until the authority answers. It also lists villages *Rejected* in the
  **Contractor** category: the authority found something you must fix.
- Rows are oldest first. The number on the right is **days in basket**; it
  turns red after 14 days.
- **· no comment** in red means nobody has written about the village in the
  last 7 days. The **No comment** tab lists all of them.

## Every save needs a comment

Pick a village and choose one tile (or press **W**, **A** or **R**):

| Choice | When | You fill in |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting · add comment** | Still waiting for ICT/CRA | What you did to follow up |
| **Approved** | The authority approved | Letter number, Shamsi date, the letter file, a comment |
| **Rejected** | The authority rejected | Rejection category, letter number, date, file, why it was rejected |
| **Fixed · back to contractor** | (on a Contractor rejection) you fixed it | What was fixed |

Press **Save · next village** (or **Ctrl Enter**). The next village opens.

Your Approved and Rejected filings go to the coordinator in **ICT/CRA
validation**. Until they confirm, the row shows **In validation** and the
case does not move. The coordinator confirms or corrects the rejection
category.

## Many villages on one letter

Tick the villages in the list. The card becomes **Update N villages**; the
choice, category, letter and comment apply to all of them.

## Keys

**J** / **K** move through the list · **W** / **A** / **R** choose ·
**Ctrl Enter** saves.
