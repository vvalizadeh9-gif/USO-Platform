# Roles Performance: Performance / My performance

*Performance for the PM and the general manager. My performance for
everyone else.*

Performance shows what one owner delivered, month by month, and how fast.
Choose the months with the range picker in the header (the last six by
default). The PM and the general manager also choose **whose**.

## Results

Four tiles: DT done, On air, ICT approved and CRA approved. Each tile shows:

* the rate and the count;
* this month's movement so far (for example "مهر: +23");
* the national rate, and the gap to it in points;
* the role average (the average owner of the same kind), and the gap to it.

**Delivered per month** shows DT done, ICT and CRA per Shamsi month. The
current month is striped, because it is not over. The dashed line is the
average ICT approvals per owner of the same kind. A month's figures belong to
whoever owned the province at the time.

Months before **Mehr 1405** show "Not recorded" for approvals.

## Activity in UEP

Shown only for people who work in UEP: contractors, coordinators and the PM.

* Filings and validations over the last five months.
* Three response times, always the **median** (the middle value, so one
  very slow case cannot drag the figure):
  1. filing to validation, for the person who validated;
  2. DT done to first ICT/CRA filing;
  3. rejection to re-filing under a new letter.

  The tick on each bar is the median across everyone in the same role.

Regional managers, provinces and CRA regions show results only, because they
do not file or validate in UEP. "Whole country" shows activity only, because
its results are the national figures.
