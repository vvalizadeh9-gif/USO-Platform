# Roles Performance: Map

*For everyone who has Roles Performance.*

The Map tab shows ICT approval by province and CRA approval by CRA region,
one map at a time. The whole map fits the window; nothing on the page scrolls.

* The PM and the general manager see the whole country.
* Everyone else sees their own provinces coloured and the rest in grey.

The colour is the share of villages that got past the stage: ICT-approved out
of drive-tested villages, and CRA-approved out of ICT-approved villages. A
province or region with fewer than **10** villages in a stage is hatched and
not coloured, for the same reason as "Not compared" on the other tabs.
Hovering a shape shows its rate, in villages and in sites.

## The detail panel

Click a province (ICT map) or a CRA region (CRA map). Click again, press
**Esc** or the close button to clear it. Every figure has two numbers, sites
and villages. A site is one work item; it counts as approved when every one of
its villages is.

* **Delivery:** total assigned (a drive-test contractor set), total on air
  (temporary or permanent launch) and total drive test, over every target
  village.
* **ICT** and **CRA:** approved and pending, over the drive-tested sites and
  villages, with the approval rate. Each village count downloads the villages
  it counts as an Excel file; site counts are plain numbers.

The owner list that used to sit under the figures is gone. Lifecycle Gaps
still lists who holds the pending villages.
