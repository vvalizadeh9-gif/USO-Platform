# Roles Performance: Map

*For everyone who has Roles Performance.*

The Map tab is the Lifecycle Gaps coverage map, unchanged. It shows ICT
approval by province and CRA approval by CRA region.

* The PM and the general manager see the whole country.
* Everyone else sees their own provinces coloured and the rest in grey.

A province or region with fewer than **10** villages in a stage is hatched
and not coloured, for the same reason as "Not compared" on the other tabs.
Clicking a shape opens its detail.
