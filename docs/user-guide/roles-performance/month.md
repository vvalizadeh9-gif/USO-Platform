# Roles Performance: Month

*For the PM and the general manager.*

Month shows what happened this month and compares it with last month.

## Reading the comparison

* **This month is compared up to today.** On 10 Mehr, Mehr 1–10 is compared
  with Shahrivar 1–10, not with the whole of Shahrivar. If last month was
  shorter, it is compared up to its last day.
* **A past month is compared whole**, against the whole month before it.
  Pick it in the month picker next to Export.

The subtitle says which comparison you are reading, for example "Day 10 of
30 · compared with شهریور up to the same day".

## The four cards

| Card | Rows | One block is |
|---|---|---|
| Project delivery | On air, DT done | 5 sites |
| Acceptance | ICT approved, CRA approved, Fully approved | 10 villages |
| Full config | Became full config, Fell back | 5 sites |
| Problematic | Resolved, New problems | 5 sites |

Each row shows this month's total with a +/− chip. Green means better than
last month and red means worse. On **Fell back** and **New problems** fewer
is better, so the colours swap. "±0" means no change.

In **Overall** each row is a row of blocks:

* **filled** blocks are this month (the last one fills to its share);
* **ringed** blocks are how far this month has gone past last month;
* **outlined, empty** blocks are how far this month is still short of last
  month.

## Owner views

**Coordinators**, **Contractors** and **Regional managers** keep the same
cards. Each row becomes a line of owners, highest first. Each owner's +/− is
against their own last month. A figure belongs to whoever owned the province
on the day it happened, so a province handed over mid-month is split between
its two owners. Anything that belongs to nobody is listed as "Unattributed",
so the owners always add up to the row.

## "Not recorded"

UEP has recorded approvals, full config and problematic changes since **Mehr
1405**. Earlier months show "Not recorded", never 0, because nothing was
written down then. On air and DT done come from CPM dates and go further back.
