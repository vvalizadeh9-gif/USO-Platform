# Roles Performance: Compare

*For the PM and the general manager only.*

Compare ranks owners of one kind against each other. It never mixes kinds.

* **Kind:** Contractors, Coordinators, Regional managers, Provinces or CRA
  regions. Each chip shows how many there are.
* **Measure:** DT done, On air, ICT or CRA. Contractors and coordinators also
  have **Speed**:
  * for contractors, the median time from DT done to the first filing;
  * for coordinators, the median time from a filing to its validation.

  Speed ranks fastest first, and its bar runs from 0 to 30 days.
* **Period:** since the start (today's standing) or one month (that month's
  events, credited to whoever owned the province at the time).

The strip at the top shows the national figure, the average owner, and the
highest and lowest.

## Ranking

Each row shows the rank and the name, a bar with a tick at the national
figure, the rate with its count, the difference from national, and the last
six months.

**Not compared.** An owner with fewer than **10 DT-done villages** (for
Speed, fewer than 10 timed cases) is listed as "Not compared". It gets no
rank, is shown in grey, and comes last.

## Months

Each owner has a lane of six months, with a bubble per month sized by the
count. The current month's bubble is dashed.

Clicking any row opens that owner in Performance.
